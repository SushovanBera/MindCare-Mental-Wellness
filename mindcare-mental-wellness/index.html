GOOD TO KNOW

Questions are welcome.

Here are a few answers to help you feel comfortable getting started.

What happens in the first session?

You'll have time to share what brings you here, ask questions and discuss what you hope to get from support. You can go at your own pace.

Can I choose online sessions?

Yes. This demo website lets you select online or in-person as a preference when requesting an appointment.

Is my information private?

Your privacy matters. This front-end demo does not send form details to a server. Do not enter sensitive personal or medical information.

Can I change my appointment later?

In a real service, contact the provider to reschedule. This demo stores requests only in your browser and does not create a real booking.
